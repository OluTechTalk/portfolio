# Log post template

One post per episode. Source: the episode's session log, DECISIONS.md entries and commits only. No invented numbers. Never include secrets, keys, store domains, IDs or env values.

---
slug: shelfready-epNN-slug

title: <plain, specific title, e.g. "Seeding 150 products, a third of them broken on purpose">
date: YYYY-MM-DD
summary: <one sentence>
pmTakeaway: <the product lesson in one line>
buildTakeaway: <the technical lesson in one line>
metric: <one real number from this episode, or omit>
commit: <link to the phase's last commit>
---

## Log post (400-700 words, for the site)
**The goal this episode.** What I set out to do and why it matters to the customer.
**The product call.** The decision or trade-off I made as a PM (scope, cost, sequencing), and what I gave up.
**What I built.** 3-5 bullets, plain language. A screenshot or diagram if there is one.
**What broke.** The surprise or bug, and how it was fixed. Readers value this section most.
**The number.** The metric from this episode, with where it came from.
**Next episode.** One line.

## LinkedIn post (150-250 words)
- Hook: first line under ~12 words, a tension or a surprise, not "Excited to share".
- 3-5 short lines: the problem → the call I made → what broke → the number.
- One lesson for PMs *and* builders.
- CTA: link to the log post (in the post, or in the first comment).
- 3 hashtags max.

## Short post (≤ 280 characters, for X / Threads / Bluesky)
One insight + link.

## Visual (optional)
Which screenshot, chart or diagram to attach.
